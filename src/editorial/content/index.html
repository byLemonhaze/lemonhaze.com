About this series

A city remembered through its textures.

Montreal began with an exploration of generative textures. Wear, stains, and marks brought back memories of old hockey socks, opening a path toward other places and moments from my life.

The series developed through restrained palettes and compositions, with personal writing embedded in each inscription. Together, the images and notes form a record of the city as I remember it.
