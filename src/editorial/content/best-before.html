Lemonhaze × ORDINALLY · 2025

BEST BEFORE

Art with a best-before date: born sealed at inscription, unsealed by its collector, and aging on block time. Many live short lives, some endure, and the rare outlier may never expire.

Where the work began

This time I more strongly realize how time is passing by. For myself and for the one I love - my family that I miss dearly. Again, so many questions... but now I have an idea. Or should I say, a "concept". I want the work to be more human and expire just like we do . Suddenly I need the chain to determine fate and keep track of time.

BEST BEFORE diary · Prelude, leading into April 29, 2025

The diary begins with mortality becoming personal. A work that expires offered a way to connect that experience to Bitcoin’s record of time. The collaboration with ORDINALLY developed the idea into a system in which the collector chooses when to begin the work’s active life.

What the collector sees

A life not yet started

The work remains sealed until its collector activates it. Its eventual visual identity and lifespan have not yet been revealed.

The collector starts the clock

A child inscription acts as the key. Activation reveals the work and determines its fate using on-chain data. Its active life is measured in Bitcoin blocks.

The living image ends

When its expiry arrives, the living render gives way to the expired state. The inscription remains on-chain. The system also allows a small possibility of a work with no scheduled expiry.

Time as part of the work

SEALED — Latent Performance A work exists in suspension. The collector chooses when to activate it. That choice—time, place, context—is already part of the artwork. UNSEALING — Act of Theater The single gesture of unsealing is a performance. Private or public, intimate or ceremonial, it is the moment the work enters time. LIFESPAN — Durational Art Once opened, the piece lives. Each Bitcoin block unfolds the performance—measured, irreversible, precise. EXPIRY — Finale Not a failure or shutdown, but a conclusion. A real ending that deserves witnessing, marking, and celebration. Bitcoin block time becomes the medium— what tape was to the banana, time itself is here.
