Originals, editions, and collection parents

Read the individual work’s details. A collection parent links a family of inscriptions; it should be identified clearly alongside the individual works. The Montreal preview includes De Sève as its collection parent.

Edition size, file format, and presentation can differ by series. For a specific work or proposed display, ask about that work directly.

Inquire about a work

Send the title or inscription link and a short description of what you have in mind—collecting, a display, or an exhibition.
